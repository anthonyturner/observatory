# Space recordings under the Review Queue's score

Real recordings made by NASA missions, played quietly under the star map's
score when its sound is on. Each is credited to NASA alone: NASA's own media
is public domain (see https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/
and https://www.jpl.nasa.gov/image-policy). Nothing credited to a university or
a partner agency is used.

Both were trimmed to a 40 second loop, low-passed, levelled to about -23 LUFS
and crossfaded at the join so the loop makes no click, then encoded as Opus.
Otherwise they are as NASA published them.

| File | Mission | Credit line | Recording | Source |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| `mars-rover-drive.opus` | Mars 2020 Perseverance | NASA/JPL-Caltech | 0:09:03 to 0:09:47 of the raw 16 minute microphone recording of the rover driving on sol 16 | https://science.nasa.gov/resource/sounds-of-perseverance-mars-rover-driving-sol-16-16-minutes/ |
| `mars-insight-seismic.opus` | InSight | NASA/JPL-Caltech | 0:22 to 1:06 of the sol 98 recording of seismometer noise (wind, the robotic arm, the seismometer's own creaks), sped up 10 times so it is audible | https://science.nasa.gov/resource/listen-to-nasas-insight-at-work-on-mars/ |

Notes on provenance:

- The Perseverance audio is the MP3 linked from its source page.
- The InSight audio file (`20190819-Sol-98-SEIS-Spatialized-reflective.wav`) is
  published on https://www.nasa.gov/sounds-from-beyond/ under the same title and
  description as its source page, which carries the credit line above.

Candidates left out because their credit line names a university or partner:
Voyager, Cassini, Juno and Van Allen Probes plasma-wave audio (University of
Iowa), the Perseverance SuperCam recordings (LANL, CNES, CNRS) and the other
InSight seismometer recordings (CNES and others).
